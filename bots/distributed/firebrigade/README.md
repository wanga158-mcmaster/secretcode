# Firebrigade evidence

Firebrigade preserves Ren as a productive local reserve and permits a bounded
intervention only for personally witnessed distress with a better complete team
continuation than the displaced independent work. Strength does not qualify it.

24 October 2026: `firebrigade/current-control` compares current default
`5dd31e2e0e9b` with `P6_CONTROL=1` (`312d25cf90dc`) against frozen Main v44 and
Fundamentals on all 40 pool maps, both sides: 320 games, no measured health flags
on either team. Forty-one of 160 paired aggregate rows differ; win outcomes do
not. This establishes an observable contrast, not intervention causality.

The selected Starfish B replay has a sampled Ren local-reserve decision but no
sampled alarm/continuation labels. Logs are sparse and cannot establish absence.
Ren's logged joint stage has a fuel denial in 47 of 79 sampled packets. Ready-state
tracks first differ for Ryth at round 103 and Ren at round 129; early telemetry
fuel also differs between builds. The aggregate contrast therefore does not
isolate Ren's alarm mechanism. Native `main.cpp` executes `organization.h`;
the extensive pure `policy.h` fixtures are a separate path.

One static applicability gap is definite: native `runtime_joint::rescue` requires
the threatened owner to carry gems, while the policy accepts empty-bag exposed
holds and blocked approaches. Neither this gap nor sampled silence proves all
alarms unreachable. The next discriminating check should separate witnessed
distress, current independent continuation, foreign owner request, fuel admission,
profitable rescue and actual selected action. An unqualified experimental opponent
may remain useful, but Firebrigade has no verified alarm/intervention contribution.
