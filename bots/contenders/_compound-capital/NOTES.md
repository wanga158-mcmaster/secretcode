## Mechanics and independent policy

Read canonical `bots/main/foundation/README.md` and economy/mechanics entries, SDK and rules.
Main v40 already has value-based recall (D115), fixed shop plan7, and strong execution. D91 and
plan100 reject treating a simple camp damage model as complete item value; preserve PvP
heal/death value. D102's larger bags and the existing hoarder120 persona are historical controls,
not justification for copying a threshold. Foundation M52 requires observed camp lifecycle,
reservation and predicted availability to remain distinct; this prototype inherits v40 beliefs.

`src/capital_model.h` is a bounded SDK-free model. Max8 local gem-bearing reachable tasks feed
damage, survival/recovery, travel, capture and respawn constraints into a sustainable portfolio
flow. Actual item candidates change that flow. Their marginal value also retains measured PvP
recovery/death savings, and Treasure's additional gold is priced through future non-Treasure
investment. The same cached gold price enters the real camp reward caller. Shop returns compare
item/heal value with actual extra productive route cost. Bank decisions retain deadline, finish,
contact and gated-route overrides, plus Marcella tribute-to-investment credit.

The item model uses immutable kit ability cooldown PERIODS through `model::camp_dps`, not live
remaining readiness. It approximates sustained future scheduled casts; it does not zero future
payoff for a temporarily unavailable cast. Whether these casts and the chosen solo workload
actually execute remains a calibration question.

Pure tests cover damage versus travel/respawn bottlenecks, PvP survival value under farming
limits, horizon0, production reward prices, Marcella income, true detour versus altar distance,
shop payback, bank overrides and stale item/purse/age credit rejection. Native events establish
that purchases and productive routes really changed; pure tests do not emulate the SDK/Brain.
