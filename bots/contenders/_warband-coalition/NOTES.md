work. No broad party assessment was launched. Preserve code/evidence and await the coordinator's

The old flock serializes production and lets mere sightings veto economic work. Its current
default loses every one of 80 full-pool keys to v40. Three separate vetoes, weak transit admission
and delayed predominantly Health purchases show an architectural gap, not a missing tuned number.
Retain useful economic work and assemble a coalition only where champions can already contribute.
The team coordinates its next damaging actions instead of making everyone travel as one ball.

The first mechanism is a common, short-lived enemy focus. `coalition_focus=false` preserves the
frozen substrate. With it enabled, `fight_enemies` can invite/adopt a target already personally
visible and in ordinary basic range while our own basic allowance is available. Other champions
self-select. A teammate's geometry/strength helps rank an invitation; it is not guaranteed future
damage. Current recall, tribute and teleport channels are excluded. The caller's existing combat
permission is preserved, including permitted in-place counterattacks; this is no new engagement
certificate. Dash/chase movement continues to use the original local focus.

The final two shared bytes encode enemy roster identity, the full absolute round and a valid
marker. Empty, malformed, future and older-than-one-round records cannot be adopted. Capacity is
checked against actual champion slot count; the feature refuses to overlap the old SOS/sighting
protocol. No location is broadcast and an unseen target cannot be chased from this record.
`coalition_trace=false` keeps bounded optional diagnostics out of measured builds.
