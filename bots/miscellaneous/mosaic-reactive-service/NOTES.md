## Why this direction exists

The old Compound field-growth implementation is preserved in full at
`bots/miscellaneous/compound-field-growth/`, including its NOTES. Its 480-game broad rejection
remains authoritative. Improved survival did not restore production. Through R400 against v42,
the replaced Ryth role collected/banked 47.375/36.425 gems per key; growth Tavi 20.225/15.775.
Growth already spent about 95% of earned gold. This does not support another shopping or Tavi
spell-preference wrapper, and it does not isolate one cause of the package's loss.

Mosaic was created with `arena new bot contenders/mosaic`, without a main/anchor `--from`.
The command supplied the six-file official starter scaffold. The experimental architecture,
model and navigation were independently written from the SDK, rules and canonical foundation;
no main or main-anchor source was inspected. Frozen v44 is a black-box opponent. The foundation
owner checked its raw and injected module identity against historical v42; old labels remain old.

The structural question is which named body's legal action transitions can turn the actual
geometry and remaining state of a camp into completed useful work. Area damage, one cast,
movement/attack allowances, persistent partial damage, individual health, capture ownership and
tribute are separate constraints. A roster, more damage, spending, or a target score alone is
not the architecture's answer. The first native discriminator deliberately tests one complete
isolated task before asserting that the ordinary selector finds profitable work.
