# Warband: independently rebuilt cooperative production

## Active state, 23 October 2026

The full inherited local-party implementation and its accumulated notes are retired intact at
`bots/miscellaneous/warband-local-party/`. Root approved that move after its activation study
closed without a competitive improvement. Its historical source identities/results remain
historical; they do not qualify this rebuild. The active path was recreated with `arena new bot
contenders/warband`, the clean frozen starter scaffold. No main or main-anchor source was read.

The new architecture treats a job as approach, harmful work, harmless cleanup, actual capture
shares, each recipient's bag conversion, release and recovery. Solo partial siege and funding are real fallback stages;
cooperation must use actual personally visible named bodies and pay for their independent work.
This is a complete controller restart, not an established stronger bot or a policy promotion.

Source ownership: coordinator owns `coop_controller.h`, `config.h`, dispatch and integration;
`coop_executor` owns `coop_micro.h`; `coop_planner` owns `coop_jobs.h`. The two headers are now
integrated and their focused pure WASI checks pass. Tests live in the corresponding owned
`.cache/contenders/warband/coop-micro/` and `coop-planner/` scratch folders.

`production_controller=false` preserves the starter control; `cooperation=true` is nested inside
the experimental controller. Draft is Marcella/Ryth/Vendetta/Vesper/Tavi as map pick count allows.
Four designated physical actors publish their own two-byte intent packets; a fifth operates
privately. The dictionary represents the first31 map groups, and unsupported groups remain solo.
Whole-round stamps do not wrap during the current2000-round game. A packet never means arrival,
survival, damage or exclusive ownership. All actual peer live reads need personal visibility;
peer gold is private and must not be queried or treated as observed zero.

The executor certifies a stationary harmful wave only, with separate HP prefixes, partial-round
readiness, equal-distance next-round priority and caller-supplied escape damage. It gives spells
no safety credit; legal basic/cast/reset/basic actions can accelerate actual work. Arrival,
outside pressure, cleanup, payout/conversion and renewal remain the macro's responsibilities.
The pure planner ranks one represented complete episode and protects distinct actor funding;
it cannot turn uncertain macro timing or route estimates into a native certificate.
