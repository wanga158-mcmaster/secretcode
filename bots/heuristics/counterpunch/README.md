# Counterpunch

Bot 14 tests one real-HP retaliation phase followed by fresh cleanup. Development
is local only; no online action or release is authorized.

Ren, Willow, Tavi, Vesper and Marcella work independently. Ren buys five Jungle
Focus shards, five Monster Health shards, then Power Focus. Other roles buy
ordinary production gear through the same queried-price purchase hook.

Before spending Ren's stun, a nonlethal basic may buy one camp phase only when
the remaining target HP lies above next-owner basic-plus-stun cleanup and within
one expected retaliation of it. Current legal kills finish immediately. Admission
requires an awake, unstunned damaging adjacent camp, no shields or visible
competing target, an available escape prefix, and current HP covering two
conservative observed incoming phases plus 40% empty/55% loaded reserve. No
future heal, shield or passive damage finances that reserve. This conservative
target heuristic does not guarantee that the camp chooses Ren.

At next owner entry actual HP and fresh action masks drive basic-first cleanup
or withdrawal. A camp lifecycle can buy only one phase. Recorded endpoint HP
changes are unattributed observed work. Wounded manageable groups and quick
captures complement harmless income fallback; ordinary bags bank at twelve,
with survival/deadline overrides and prompt conversion of a personally paid
Counterpunch capture. The cast-order arm is separately controlled by
`COUNTERPUNCH_ORDER`; its off arm retains the same roster, shopping and source
policy. This is a commitment and fresh cleanup experiment, not an absorption
renewal controller, an act-first planning scheduler or a specialist admission
program. Basic damage and spell metadata, camp design/range, growth and prices
are queried. Expected retaliation omits a possible Monster bonus conservatively;
raw incoming camp Strength bounds the HP reserve even after Jungle mitigation.

The executor keeps the complete static walking component, validates the actual
origin after recall/respawn, and measures stable-goal progress using native field
distance. Eight quiet route turns can retire a source after sixty no-work turns.
The persistent global no-work clock survives movement, source switches and recall.
After 180 quiet turns, Renewal quarantines the source and allows at most two of
its own safe empty-bag recall attempts until actual work or personal income.
Other existing recovery paths are not universally capped. Public designs are
cached per camp kind. Capture uses all members plus an elapsed-hold watchdog;
tribute/recall retain their real channel lifecycle and fresh action checks.

The first fixed natural screen reached two arms, one cleanup and one withdrawal.
Independent review then repaired a fresh-enemy cleanup veto and a successful
basic receipt that could be lost when the post-action exposure check declined.
Those developmental results are superseded. The next health review found a real
spawn loop: Vesper on Rushbait and Willow on Reverse Spiral repeatedly healed at
owner end, then received enemy damage before the next owner entry. A strict
full-HP gate kept both motionless for the whole game. `SpawnRest` now rests only
on an actual arrival turn. The fixed four-key regression restores all sixteen
actors' personal income; the former zero-income Willow collects/submits 140 gems.
The suspected component-fuel admission failure did not explain these cases and
was not used to justify unrelated changes.

Native inspection also found that an actual four-gem counterpunch capture did
not promptly activate the deferred payout watcher. `PaidCapture` now records
actual selected-pad residence after all own actions and checks the next entry's
own currency delta plus fresh selected-source respawning state before ordinary
source replacement/recall exits. Source death and shared ally income alone are
insufficient. An observed completed lifecycle without personal payment retires
the old expectation; a source switch alone cannot erase a valid prior hold.
Bank priority is cleared by actual bag conversion or death. A priority flag does
not establish quick travel, safe conversion or a causal economic benefit.
