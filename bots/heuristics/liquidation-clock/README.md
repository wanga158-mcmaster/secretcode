# Liquidation Clock

Bot 15 fixes a whole-game conversion reserve, then irreversibly stops ordinary
gem production per actor. Its terminal flag survives empty bags, banks, death and
respawn. The threshold is a heuristic, not an impossibility proof: remaining
owner-start rounds at most `max(24, 2*capture_time + D(max(1,bag)))`, with queried
`D(n)=max(minimum,base+ceil(n*multiplier))`.

The fixed roster is Tavi, Willow, Vesper, Marcella, Vendetta. Ordinary work favors
nearby short remaining clear/capture/conversion with an observed crowd penalty.
Twelve-gem batches have near-altar and wounded overrides. Finance uses Monster
Focus for Tavi and Monster Strength otherwise, then Jungle Health; mature Tavi
buys Focus, Willow Health, others Strength. Terminal actors buy no optional gear.
Spent gold still counts toward the lifetime earned-gold tiebreak.

At terminal, preserve a safe actual channel first; loaded actors convert through
the fresh bank executor. An empty actor may finish only its already-selected,
physically held cleared reward, under the elapsed CaptureWatch, and must observe
actual personal payout before claiming conversion. Then empty actors pursue safe
zero-gem gold sources whose coarse actual payout ETA fits. All source members
must die and physical capture must finish. Unknown existing capture progress may
pay next phase. Loaded capture/tribute overlap never lengthens a running tribute.

The local executor carries repaired SpawnRest, PaidCapture, RouteWatch, complete
static walking components and bounded Renewal mechanics from 13/14, without
Counterpunch's exposure strategy. Action receipts are separate from accepted plans.
