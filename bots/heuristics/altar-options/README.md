# Altar options

Root's first durable placement is `.cache/arena/ladder/20261022-024058`: eighteen
subject games, 15-3, all clean. The two flagged games in the 48-game run are
Bargainer stalls outside heuristics. Nearest coarse style is
`specialists/reservoir-wardens`, distance 0.51, without head-to-head; no
convergence flag. This sparse, unbalanced placement does not reverse the
weaker same-run paired result.

Bot 09 owns a private service program for its existing positive cargo. It
inspects an altar, removes its hostile lock or diverts to one alternative,
starts tribute only after fresh visible absence of a hostile lock, and waits
for actual conversion before returning to independent production. There is no
team service contract, circuit learning, hostile channel hunt, escort or ward
information experiment. It buys no locks.

The roster is Marcella, Tavi, Vesper, Varg and Ryth, in that order, supporting
three to five picks. Public terrain approach fields are prepared one per
bounded setup turn. A new cargo commitment shortlists at most two reachable
altars. Remaining successful basic hits, current attack/reset allowance,
ranged arrival restrictions and route/inspection work price the alternatives.
Strength does not reduce lock hits. Hidden lock state stays unknown; the
alternative is an inspection option rather than a claimed unlocked altar.

The controller retains useful removal instead of rescoring every turn. New
obstruction, newly visible alternate state, unsafe exposure, three failed
actions, six rounds without real distance/hit progress or an absolute deadline
can reopen the choice. Spent hits are sunk; a two-turn switching friction
discourages churn. Failed alternatives are quarantined for that attempt;
abandonment gives ordinary production eight rounds before another commitment.
One tick performs at most two basics and one successful reset. The SDK adapter
re-queries enemy lock snapshots after every hit, and waits until a later owner
turn after the final hit before submitting. A submit call alone is not a
channel receipt. Existing recall, tribute and teleport channels are preserved.

An owned channel followed by a living empty bag and a sufficient score increase
confirms a conversion. Death, interruption and zero cargo have explicit state
cleanup. Non-acting lifecycle observation precedes recovery, channel and dead
owner returns. No forced service state can require an empty bag to bank.
Ordinary cleared-source capture still has priority over servicing older cargo.

Independent production selects a financing lot: prefer a survivable source
which can fund the next useful equipment purchase, then minimise work per
useful currency and crowding. Once already funded, or mature, useful currency
becomes gems. Only Marcella's own converted gems count as prospective gold.
The actual shop updates the owned policy's next-price field from the queried
item cost. This targets one next purchase; field-to-shop travel is deliberately
batched at six queried prices rather than recalling for every shard. Tavi
shops in the field. Vesper buys Monster Focus, other workers Monster Strength;
Varg then buys Jungle Strength, others Jungle Health. Later Vesper buys Power
Focus, Marcella/Ryth Power Health, and Tavi/Varg Power Strength. These plans
drive actual purchases. Cargo raises the personal life reserve.

The executor locally reuses the suite's bounded metadata, capture watchdog,
observed-dead frontier memory and safe blocked-exit mechanics. It contains no
08 auction or handshake. A bounded source work ticket keeps actual distance
or damage progress for at most ninety rounds and expires after twelve stagnant
rounds. Varg refuses splash that wakes another dangerous source. The top turn
boundary catches unexpected errors, logs a line containing `error`, and yields.

The experiment began with `HEURISTICS_ENABLED=0`. The healthy active default is
now retained as a distinct experimental losing controller. Explicit off includes
frozen common Fundamentals without new state. This is not a release candidate
or a strength improvement, and no online command is part of this work.

The lesson for 10 is to distinguish an information change from the consequence
it enables. Monolith's own visible enemy movement changes an executable service
choice and leads to actual personal conversion; the hidden alternative was not
known unlocked in advance. A future information probe needs its own observed
decision change and continuation, rather than credit for an omniscient map
fact. Fresh snapshots, a successful action and a receipt remain separate facts.
Remaining lock work is discrete basic attacks; ordinary Strength purchases do
not make a lock cheaper. Failed alternatives need useful production escape,
and bounded progress must survive interruption and zero cargo. Keep these
lessons without turning each later bot into this same service controller.
