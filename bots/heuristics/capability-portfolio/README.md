# Capability Portfolio

Bot 13 tests capability admission followed by a small role interpreter. A
public terrain summary chooses at most one specialist. Other workers clear,
capture and bank independently; no role waits for a teammate's first income.

Access admits Vesper only for a valuable source outside the home walking
component, with a reversible two-step crossing over a hole, all-member work
access and a home-component banking suffix. The program approaches the exact
entry, freshly validates and crosses, clears and physically holds the source,
returns over the reserved crossing and converts any gem cargo. A failed cast never
advances the phase. Occupancy, exposure, deadline or missing capacity refusal
leads to bounded ordinary production, with safe recall as an explicit exit.
This implementation does not admit same-component walking detours.

Supply substitutes Tavi for Vendetta when a productive connected region is at
least fourteen walking steps from the spawn shop and within ten steps of a
usable altar. A selected capture must fund one queried Focus-shard price, and
the queried Focus growth must reduce a coarse spell-hit threshold on another
reachable source. After that actual capture, Tavi buys exactly one field shard
through the policy purchase hook, re-queries actual capacity and does useful
work before converting cargo. Ordinary Tavi cannot auto-buy in the field.
Field Health is never treated as healing.

Gold-only sources may qualify: their capital can fund permanent equipment.
A zero-cargo Bank phase completes immediately; it is not gem conversion.

The ordinary roster is Vesper, Vendetta and Marcella. Supply uses Vesper, Tavi
and Marcella. Fourth/fifth picks are independent Varg and Willow. Draft memory
is a complete zero-filled 1024-byte, versioned and checksummed envelope written
before each valid pick. Runtime binds it to actual roster and SDK source
identities; invalid or partial plans reconstruct ordinary roles.

`HEURISTICS_ENABLED=0` compiles only frozen common/control. The nested
`PORTFOLIO_PROGRAMS=0` diagnostic keeps actual draft picks but disables the
special interpreter and field threshold purchase. It is an ablation rather
than a second production controller.
