# Witnessed handoff

Bot 08 asks whether a fixed clearer can leave a freshly cleared source to a
personally visible holder and begin other useful production before the payout.
Varg clears; Marcella and Tavi are the next two draft choices, followed by
Vendetta and Willow. These are suite draft indexes, not physical SDK identities.
Every actor remains an independent worker. The solo completion auction prices
its own expected combat loss, nearby competing workers, observed certainty and
the trip needed to convert gems. It has no learned route program.

Strength purchases immediately shorten ordinary clears. After five Monster
Strength shards, Varg buys Jungle Strength while holders buy Jungle Health;
later Power purchases follow those roles. The actual shop uses the policy hook,
queried costs and current buy legality. A donation requires a real own payout
and a purchased upgrade. After actual departure Varg must receive a different
source's own payout before donating again. There is no compulsory long capital
target before ordinary banking: twelve gems, a nearby altar, danger, an absolute
deadline or a periodic conversion window can trigger it.

## Production contract

There is at most one eight-byte team packet, explicitly packed into a uint64.
The key is canonical public member tile plus one, clearer physical type, holder
physical type and offer round. The canonical tile is an actual minimum spawn
tile rather than a centroid. Fields include phase, short report offset and an
absolute deadline. Types, map resolution, time representation and capacity are
validated; invalid packets fall back to solo work. Whole-packet writes reread
live host bytes in a short fuel-reserved helper and verify the current round.

Only the clearer's own successful action can authorize an offer: the group was
alive immediately before the action and reward-available immediately afterward
in the same round, with the clearer physically on a member tile. At least two
usable pads, a visible nearby holder and an independently affordable, visible
living outside source are required. Holders check their own health, cargo,
channel and current capture commitment. Acceptance is an intent report. Varg
requires a fresh matching report and personally visible physical holder on a
source pad before moving. Release is published only after actual position is
outside the entire source. A successful step along a long source is insufficient.

The holder has its own elapsed reward-hold watchdog, independent of packet
cancellation and enemy visibility. Personal payout requires continuous physical
hold, reward-to-respawning transition and actual own bag or gold gain sampled
before shopping. A fresh matching release is synchronized before attributing a
receipt when payout precedes the holder's next dispatch. The holder reports PAID
and banks its own bag. Varg attempts the saved outside source for at most 35
rounds, then returns to the solo auction while the income debt remains. Absolute
contract deadlines continue through recovery, banking and death. No teammate
gold query or promised heal is used.

## Mechanical safeguards and checks

The local executor copies bounded setup, public topology, remembered occupancy,
frontier exploration, static altar goals and capture escape primitives. It
contains neither 07's Circuit brain nor 06's shield duty phases. Varg refuses
ordinary splash into a different dangerous source and temporarily skips that
source so refusal cannot become an endless local orbit. A blocked expired hold
can move, bank adjacent cargo, attack one harmless whole-group blocker or safely
recall. Death and actual lifecycle change reset the hold; packet cancellation
does not. The top turn catches unexpected exceptions, logs `error` and yields.

without new bookkeeping. Mutable two-actor fixtures run the same Controller and
dispatch as the SDK adapter, including both actor orders, delayed reports,

expiry, stale keys, death, renewed sources, failed exits and receipt integrity.

The experiment demonstrates reachable, funded division of work and outside
production before the holder's payout, while the overall bundle still loses.
Nearby assembly, hidden contests, shared damage, interrupted recovery and
outside routing can consume the gain. A PAID report remains a teammate report,
and a legal release is not exclusive ownership. The next controller should
price coupled commitments and terminal resource states explicitly; actual
personal receipts and zero-cargo continuations matter more than promised
production. Preserve these lessons without copying this auction and handshake
into every later bot. Work stops at 08.
