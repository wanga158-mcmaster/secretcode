# Act First

Bot 11 executes a fresh safe saved incumbent before doing one rotating cached
scalar challenger audit. The audit can propose an integer source for the next
owner turn only. Fresh life, finance, capture, banking, channel and safety
vetoes precede adoption; a failed physical action cannot publish a proposal.

Development started with `HEURISTICS_ENABLED=0`, retaining frozen Fundamentals.
The active executor owns routing, risk, finance and role equipment. This is an
execution-order experiment; it does not run a full global source feature/rank
prefix before the saved intent's action.

## Procedure and interacting heuristics

`act.h` is the production controller, exercised directly by the pure adapter
fixture. `runtime.h` supplies the fresh SDK prefix. Public source handles,
member coordinates and rewards are cached once. Bootstrap considers at most
eight cheap public candidates and can act on its first gameplay turn; no
complete per-source design setup is required. A steady turn
details only immediate safety and the selected source. The selected source
retains every personally visible empty member pad, including when another
member is alive, and expires that memory on its source's queried respawn time.

Fresh life, channels, recovery, bag/gold/gear epoch, urgent banking and capture
continuity precede proposals. A teammate's final hit can create a first capture
hold between owner turns: an on-incumbent-pad reward check establishes that
hold before proposal adoption, even when the watchdog has not started yet.
Capture elapsed time survives empty enemy vision and target cancellation. An
expired hold tries a physical departure, useful bank or safe recall; failed
departure does not reset its elapsed time.

The actual action returns its outcome. A hidden `move_absolute=false` cannot
authorize an audit. A successful saved-source hit or step can authorize one
rotating challenger comparison, using only already cached scalar metadata and
observations. The audit performs no SDK bitboard/field/source/vision enumeration,
sorting or logging, and creates no SDK handles in its proposal. The proposal
contains integer source/owner/life/finance stamps and completion. It is eligible
on exactly the next owner turn, then expires on adoption or veto. Gold-only
payouts and purchases advance the same finance epoch as cargo changes.

The scalar policy prices cashable throughput, purchase financing, unknown work,
capture/travel time, bag-sensitive risk and switching friction. Fresh safety
overrides it. Recent best-distance progress vetoes switching for four rounds;
the no-camp-work age survives movement and source alternation. Sixty turns
without work trigger reconsideration only together with eight rounds of route
stagnation; six failed steps independently trigger a short source refusal.
Actual camp work, payout or a new life reset the physical age.

The audit admission is 4,000 fuel plus a 24,000 normal tail: skip before *any*
audit input if fewer than 28,000 remain. Round changes or a fuel refill invalidate
the result without unsigned subtraction or publication. The mandatory route
admits one native field at 60,000 fuel or uses at most eight fresh neighbors
with a short failed-neighbor memory. Greedy movement is not a detour guarantee.
The cheap route returns to a separate minimal yield tail, bypassing the audit,
receipt queries and formatted logging. Normal bounded event receipts are outside
the audit and require 40,000 remaining fuel. `run()` directly yields after
`turn()`; it has no unconditional receipt/perception work after a low-fuel return.

One complete static-terrain walking-component flood is a mandatory capture
admission certificate. It is admitted at 80,000 fuel before selected-source
perception and forces the bounded neighbor continuation rather than a second
native routing field that turn. Each selected source lazily caches whether any
capture pad is in that component. Dynamic source/vision/risk ranking is not
part of the flood. The flood has no 256-distance cutoff: routing's transient
256 cap cannot prove permanent disconnection. A fresh origin outside the cached
component, including after recall or respawn, starts a new component epoch and
invalidates old source access stamps. This proves necessary terrain access,
not hidden occupancy, safe combat, successful movement or eventual arrival.

Shopping queries actual prices and `can_buy` and executes the same pure
`Policy::purchase` hook tested by the profile corpus: role-based Monster
Strength/Focus, Varg Jungle Strength versus Jungle Health for other roles, then
role-based Power Focus/Strength/Health. Banking uses small twelve-gem batches,
near-altar conversion, injury and deadline overrides. Recovery and active
capture remain protected before finance travel.

## Development failures and bounded repairs

The first twelve-key full-game smoke, `h12-11-smoke`, exposed three
`Champion cannot act` errors against Tempo on Ambush. A fresh all-allowance
veto before shop/action handling removed the stunned-turn calls. The next exact
smoke was technically clean, with maximum ordinary fuel 52,956.

The first eighty-key all-pool Starter screen, `h12-11-liveness`, had no faults,
exhaustion or errors but five raw zero-clear flags. Native inspection found
Rows A/B had real captures and banks (16/31 gems, 447/400 earned gold), while
Boss Battle A/B and Concentrated Wealth A had no income. All five were kept
separate from final evidence. The initial age gate retired each newly selected
source after one move once age reached sixty; private age was never cleared,
so all five actors could cycle indefinitely with ample fuel. Gating that
retirement on physical route stagnation restored Concentrated Wealth. A second
repair preserves a physically improving approach against challenger churn;
`h12-11-progress-friction`, six keys on Rows/Boss Battle/Concentrated Wealth,
then completed 4/6 with zero raw zero-clear or technical flags, maximum 43,518.

The hypothesis that all-visible selected-group incoming caused the Boss Battle
failure was inspected in two bounded native windows and remained unproved.
Combat/risk thresholds were left unchanged. No unsafe combat relaxation or
map-specific exception was used. These repairs establish liveness for the
tested bundle; their economic contributions are not isolated causal effects.

Read-only review also found and repaired two proposal-freshness holes before
the final freeze: gold-only cash changes did not invalidate finance stamps,
and a teammate-created first hold could otherwise be skipped by adoption.
The latter's exact SDK transition is covered by the reviewed production order
and fresh capture-veto contract, not a full guest-branch replay fixture.

The first complete paired pool run `h12-11-final`, active `79ffd5e83fd8`, was
technically clean but still had five no-income Islands cells and one productive
raw zero-clear Corridors B cell. Native late Islands windows showed full health,
fresh movement/basic/cast allowances and no channel or immediate threat. Exact
public terrain connectivity puts 38 of 46 groups on isolated components.
A single bounded `HEURISTICS_TRACE=1` diagnostic recorded thirty idle frames at
rounds 860–865: cached selected goals were unreachable, recovery was false and
84–87K fuel remained. Trace-off snapshot `36707a9ac8c1` preserved the measured
normal raw bytes (`4dbec533986a`); the diagnostic's changed trajectory alone is
not treated as a normal-prefix reproduction.

The complete static capture-admission repair then restored ordinary production
in `h12-11-capture-access`: sixteen normal keys on Islands, Big World, Collision
and Ambush, both sides versus Fundamentals/Tempo, zero technical or raw zero
flags, 8/16 wins, maximum ordinary fuel 55,427. This validates the necessary
admission repair on those keys without claiming every old stationary moment
has been reconstructed. The full final rerun uses the frozen repaired source
`471b85617f41`; all earlier pool/legacy outcomes and native chains remain
separate, superseded development evidence.

## Final exact-source evidence and decision

**Accepted as a distinct experimental losing controller**, enabled for the
suite's diversity goal, not as a strength improvement or release candidate.
Frozen repaired active `471b85617f41` completed
`.cache/arena/sweeps/h12-11-final-access`: 40 pool maps, both sides, four
opponents, 320 identical keys per arm / 640 games. Active 102/320 versus frozen
control 162/320; flips +11/-71, exact McNemar p=6.81e-12, paired gem-margin
difference -335.1 +/- 24.5 SE. Main was exactly one quarter (0/80 active wins).
Other active records were 7/80 against Fundamentals, 28/80 against Tempo and
67/80 against Concentric. This is a strongly negative bundle result.

All 320 active pool games had zero faults, exhaustion, error lines and
at-budget turns; maximum ordinary fuel 55,427. The two raw zero-clear flags
remain explicitly reported: Swirl A and Hooks B against Main. Native complete
tracks show active combat losses, not stationary planning failures. Swirl ends
around 820 with all four actors having 241–319 movement transitions and 7–9
deaths each; Hooks ends at 1040 with 244–298 moves and 11–12 deaths each. All
actors dealt positive monster/champion damage, with live actors still moving
or newly respawned at the end. Classification:
`.cache/act-first-review/native/h12-11-final-access-raw-zero-summary.json`.
The control retains eight raw stall flags and is preserved for byte equality.

Fresh confirmation `.cache/arena/runs/h12-11-final-access-confirm` completed
100/102 on seventeen legacy maps, both sides, against Starter, Banked Vanguard
and Bargainer. All 102 subject games had zero technical, at-budget or raw
zero-clear flags; maximum ordinary fuel 52,013. The exact active source is the
same as the pool arm. These wins establish transfer against this weaker panel,
not strength against Main or a positive causal effect of the audit.

`.cache/act-first-review/native/access-native-summary.json`, SHA256
`21fdd0cb98845957a88341a2a6f52b5eebe8f5be599a4d3ac02dfe4aa5e2609d`.

ordinary personal production, not a causal benefit from Tavi's audit, a
same-actor audit-to-payout chain or a solo-clear claim. No search for an economic
positive was extended beyond these two bounded windows.

The completed 320-key pool arm retained 46,959 normal production receipts.
`.cache/act-first-review/native/h12-11-final-access-fuel-tail.json` measures
